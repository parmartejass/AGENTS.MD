# Governance Learning

Jurisdiction: session-evidenced governance-learning candidates, their promotion, and bounded session-log evidence acquisition.

## Promotion

- Only an evidenced reusable governance gap with a concrete prevention point MAY be promoted; reject task-local, temporary, weakly evidenced, or owner-conflicting candidates with reason.
- Each candidate MUST carry its evidence and explicit status.
- A promoted candidate MUST take the smallest enforcing form: existing-asset extension, skill, hook, subagent, or none.

## Session-log evidence acquisition

- Acquisition collects evidence only; promotion, rejection, and edits stay outside it.
- Acquisition MUST return one explicit outcome that distinguishes complete-not-found from partial or failed search.
